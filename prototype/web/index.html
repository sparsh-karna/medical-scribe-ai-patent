Three checks, not one.

Ambient AI scribes already link a note back to the transcript. That alone does not stop an error from entering the record. This system adds enforcement on top of the link.

01

Dual-signal grounding

Every drafted statement is scored against the acoustic confidence of the recognizer and the semantic overlap with the exact transcript segment it claims to come from. Either signal failing blocks the statement outright.

02

Adaptive calibration

When a clinician resolves a blocked statement, the threshold for that field shifts toward their judgment, field by field. The safety floors underneath never move.

03

Clinical-plausibility check

A drafted medication is cross-checked against the patient's own documented allergies and against basic antibiotic-stewardship rules before it can auto-populate the chart.

Validated on real consultations, not invented examples.

Every result shown after sign-in runs on PriMock57, 57 recorded primary-care consultations performed by clinicians for research use (Babylon Health, CC-BY 4.0). You can also paste or upload your own transcript.
